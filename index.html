QueueLess is a web-based virtual queue management system designed to help outpatient clinics manage patient queues while giving patients greater visibility over their waiting time.

Get Started ↓

HOW QUEUELESS WORKS

Your queue.
Your time.

QueueLess allows patients to join and monitor their clinic queue without spending unnecessary time waiting in the waiting area.

01

Join

Join the queue online or scan the clinic's daily QR code when you arrive.

02

Track

View your queue number, current position and estimated waiting time.

03

Know

Receive a notification when your turn is approaching.

04

Check In

Arrive at the clinic and confirm your presence before your consultation.

WHY QUEUELESS

Waiting shouldn't
mean wondering.

QueueLess gives patients better visibility over their waiting time while helping clinics manage their daily queues more efficiently.

01

Less Physical Waiting

Patients can join the queue remotely instead of spending their entire waiting period inside the clinic.

02

Know Your Position

Patients can see their queue number, position and estimated waiting time.

03

Better Management

Clinic staff can manage the queue, call patients and record no-shows through one dashboard.
